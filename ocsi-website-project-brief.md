**Document status:** Initial project brief, subject to stakeholder

## 1. Project Overview

### Project objective

Design and develop a professional, responsive corporate website for
Optimized Customer Solutions, Inc. (OCSI), a Philippine FMCG
distribution company. The website will strengthen OCSI's corporate
identity, establish credibility with business stakeholders, and present
its capabilities, geographic footprint, and people.

The website will serve as OCSI's official digital presence, giving
prospective business partners, existing stakeholders, job seekers, and
the general public a reliable source of company information.

### Project at a glance

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Primary goal                        Corporate branding, credibility,
                                      and a consistent professional
                                      online presence

  Platform                            WordPress with a responsive,
                                      mobile-first design

  Key audiences                       Business partners, brand
                                      principals, retailers, job seekers,
                                      and other stakeholders

  Core features                       Branch directory, business inquiry
                                      forms, CMS-managed job vacancies

  Estimated delivery                  6--8 weeks, assuming timely
                                      approvals and a controlled initial
                                      scope
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Business objectives

1.  Establish a credible and professional digital presence for OCSI.
2.  Communicate the company's background, capabilities, values, and
    distribution network.
3.  Make verified company and branch information easy to access.
4.  Generate legitimate business inquiries through a structured contact
    workflow.
5.  Support recruitment through CMS-managed job vacancies.
6.  Allow authorized staff to update website content without developer
    involvement.

### Success indicators

-   All priority pages are published and approved before launch.
-   Responsive behavior is validated across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
-   Inquiry forms are tested, with submissions reaching designated
    recipients.
-   HR staff can create, update, and close job vacancies.
-   Branch information is accurate and easy to find.
-   Search indexing, analytics, backups, and basic security are
    configured.

-   Leadership or management introduction, if approved

### 4.3 Our Capabilities

**Purpose:** Show the business's operational value to prospective
partners.

Recommended sections:

1.  Capabilities overview
2.  Distribution operations
3.  Sales and market execution
4.  Logistics, warehousing, and inventory handling
5.  Territory coverage and delivery capabilities
6.  Quality, service standards, and operational processes, if documented
7.  Business partnership CTA

This can launch as a single page with anchored sections. Add separate
service pages only if OCSI has distinct, substantial service offerings
that warrant their own content.

### 4.4 Our Network

**Purpose:** Make OCSI's geographic presence understandable and
demonstrate its ability to serve relevant markets.

Recommended sections:

-   Introduction to the distribution network
-   Coverage map or regional overview
-   Branch directory
-   Search or filter by region, province, or city, depending on network
    size
-   Individual branch details
-   General contact CTA

Each branch record should support:

-   Branch name
-   Address
-   Region or service area
-   Official phone number and email, if applicable
-   Operating hours, if relevant and confirmed
-   Map or directions link
-   Optional facility image

The branch directory should be CMS-managed. A custom map integration is
optional, not a launch requirement.

### 4.5 Our Partners

**Purpose:** Reinforce credibility with verified business relationships
and recognitions.

Recommended sections:

-   Partnership overview
-   Approved brand or principal logos
-   Explanation of OCSI's role in the distribution ecosystem
-   Awards and recognition
-   Business partnership inquiry CTA

Only display partner logos and award claims that OCSI has approved for
public use. The site must not imply a partnership or endorsement that
has not been authorized.

### 4.6 Careers

**Purpose:** Establish OCSI as an employer and make recruitment
opportunities easy to find.

Recommended sections:

-   Employer branding hero
-   Introduction to the workplace and company culture
-   Employee benefits, only where confirmed
-   Working environment and employee photos
-   Current openings
-   Application process
-   Recruitment FAQs
-   Link to the designated recruitment contact

#### Recommended application workflow

Applicants click Apply, complete an approved application form or follow
an official application link, and receive a confirmation. HR manages
vacancy publication and closure in WordPress.

Do not store CVs and applicant records in WordPress by default. If file
uploads are necessary, define access controls, retention, consent, and
privacy procedures first.

### 4.7 Contact Us

**Purpose:** Give visitors a dependable way to contact the appropriate
team.

#### Recommended form fields

-   Full name
-   Work email or preferred email
-   Company name, if applicable
-   Inquiry category
-   Contact number, optional unless required
-   Message
-   Privacy notice acknowledgment, where appropriate

Inquiry categories can include general inquiries, business partnerships,
and other corporate inquiries. Recruitment inquiries should route to the
Careers page or designated HR channel.

#### Additional content

-   Official headquarters contact details
-   General company email and phone number
-   Office address and map link
-   Links to the branch directory
-   Social links, if official accounts exist

Form submissions should be routed to designated business inboxes, with
spam protection and a clear success or failure state.

## 6. Design Direction

### Visual identity

Use clean layouts, strong typography, generous whitespace, restrained
color use, and consistent spacing. The website should look professional
and established without feeling generic or overly promotional.

### Photography

Prioritize authentic OCSI assets:

-   Staff and leadership
-   Distribution and warehouse operations
-   Delivery vehicles and facilities
-   Sales and field operations
-   Branch locations
-   Employee and workplace photography

Use stock photography only as a temporary substitute or where authentic
photography is unavailable. Confirm usage rights for all images and
logos.

### Design principles

-   Mobile-first and responsive
-   Clear visual hierarchy
-   Readable typography and accessible contrast
-   Consistent button and form styles
-   Fast-loading pages
-   Realistic, credible imagery
-   Clear calls to action without excessive animation

### Content and brand dependencies

Before visual design is finalized, request:

-   Official logo files and brand guidelines
-   Approved colors and typography, if established
-   Company profile and corporate messaging
-   Approved photographs and image usage rights
-   Authorized partner logos
-   Verified branch list
-   Official contact information
-   Approved awards and milestones

    sizes.

## Project Summary

OCSI should launch with a focused, professionally designed WordPress
corporate website that communicates its identity and capabilities,
presents a trustworthy branch directory, generates business inquiries,
and allows HR to manage vacancies independently.

The recommended initial scope is intentionally limited to the features
that support corporate credibility and recruitment. Additional portals,
integrations, and advanced functionality should be considered only after
the initial website's needs and ownership model are established.

**Planning estimate:** 6--8 weeks, subject to discovery, content
readiness, technical dependencies, and stakeholder approvals.
